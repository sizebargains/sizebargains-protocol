# Size Whitepaper

## Vision

Large memecoin positions are awkward to move. Selling an entire stack into a pool drags the price against the seller, and buying one pushes it against the buyer. Size exists so that a buyer and a holder can agree a figure for an entire position directly and swap it in one step, with no pool involved.

## Mission

Give holders another way to find liquidity, and give buyers a way to ask for size without telling the market. Keep every deal visible to the two people in it, and keep every step checkable on Solana.

## How a deal works

1. **Propose.** A buyer picks a stack and attaches a figure. The proposal is addressed to the wallet holding the stack.
2. **Agreed.** The holder reviews it from their own key and accepts, counters, or lets it lapse.
3. **Delivered.** Both sides sign once. Tokens and payment swap inside a single transaction.

## Roadmap

### Phase 1: Site and board
Public site, live board of the busiest Solana coins, worked example driven by live liquidity.

### Phase 2: Contract and identity
Publish the contract address, open the official channels, and release the specification of the deal format.

### Phase 3: First deals
Open proposals and acceptances on a limited list of coins, with settlement checked against holder balances.

### Phase 4: Open board
Any Solana coin can be listed, with holder lookup and a history of closed deals.
